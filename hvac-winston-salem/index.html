Complete Heating & Cooling Services for Winston-Salem Properties

We service every type of heating and cooling equipment you'll find in local homes and businesses, from central air conditioners and gas furnaces to heat pumps and ductless mini-splits. It doesn't matter whether the building is a starter home, a condo, or a busy office. Our technicians understand the local weather, the age and construction of local homes, and the code requirements inspectors enforce, which pays off in the quality of every repair.

A typical week for our residential team might include capacitor and contactor replacements, flame sensor cleanings, refrigerant leak searches, blower motor swaps, plus the occasional full system replacement or ductless install. When we do replace equipment, we size it with a room-by-room load calculation rather than matching the old unit. A correctly sized system keeps rooms even, keeps utility bills reasonable, and avoids the short-cycling that wears out compressors early. Beyond repairs and replacements, we recommend seasonal tune-ups that clean coils, confirm the refrigerant charge, test safety controls, and measure static pressure, the best single step for a longer-lasting system.

Commercial work is a big part of what we do as well. We service rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment for restaurant kitchens, shops, offices, and multi-unit buildings, and we know that every hour of downtime hurts the business. Quick Heating and Cooling Help in Winston-Salem

When your heat or air quits, how fast help shows up matters more than anything else. That's why we keep scheduling flexible across the area and put a real person on the phone. Emergency calls usually get a technician within a day, and we treat no-heat and no-cool calls as top priority.

Here's what we see most often when something breaks at home: warm air from the AC, a condenser that hums but won't start, a furnace that lights for a few seconds and quits, a breaker that keeps tripping, an evaporator coil that has iced over, a condensate drain backed up into the furnace cabinet, or a thermostat that has quit. Nearly all of these come down to parts already on our trucks: capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and common fittings. You get the price before any work starts, with no surprises on the invoice.

Two common situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: switch the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can flood the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and give us a call. That small step can prevent a much larger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Winston-Salem

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
