Kitchen Climate Control

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Wilson HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we plan visits around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected HVAC equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling fast, throughout Wilson and the surrounding area.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Wilson HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation before peak demand.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Clients who switch to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to talk through a plan sized to your property and budget.
