The HVAC Team Wilson Homeowners Rely On

Your home is the biggest investment you'll ever make, and a failing HVAC system undermines your family's comfort every day it goes unaddressed. Left alone, a cracked heat exchanger or a clogged condensate drain can turn a comfort complaint into a safety hazard or a water-damage repair. When that happens, you need a team that responds quickly, diagnoses the fault accurately, and repairs it properly. That's exactly what Wilson HVAC delivers.

Our trucks carry the most common failure parts, including capacitors, contactors, igniters, and fan motors, so most routine repairs wrap up on the first visit, whether the system is a gas furnace, a split AC, a heat pump, or a ductless mini-split.

Clear Pricing

Honest, Upfront Pricing

Before we pick up a wrench, we find out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians check your system's age and tonnage, the refrigerant charge, the heat exchanger and coils, and the condition of your ductwork. Then you get an honest recommendation and the complete price up front, whether the job is swapping a worn capacitor or replacing the whole system. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons.

Many HVAC problems stay hidden until a failure is well underway. Refrigerant seeps out through pinhole leaks in the evaporator coil, blower bearings wear slowly, and a weakening capacitor gives no warning until the compressor won't start on the first hot day. If you notice weak airflow, rising energy bills, ice on the refrigerant lines, or rooms that never reach the thermostat setting, schedule an inspection sooner rather than later. Catching it early often keeps a repairable issue from becoming a replacement.

Not every visit is about a breakdown. Many Wilson homeowners ask us to evaluate an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, a replacement on your own timeline beats a rushed swap in the middle of a heat wave. We'll give you a straight answer on how much life your equipment has left.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Maintenance Plans for Wilson Homes

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service heads off failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still cheap to fix. At Wilson HVAC, every plan is built around your home's actual equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check across the duct system.

Every maintenance plan we write is a little different. An older furnace gets a closer look than a brand-new heat pump, and a dusty, high-pollen area means condenser coils foul faster. We build spring cooling and fall heating visits around your equipment and climate, so every tune-up keeps the system efficient and protects the equipment you've already paid for.

The savings add up quickly. Clean coils move heat with less effort, a correct refrigerant charge takes strain off the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Compared with the cost of replacing a system even one year early, a maintenance plan is a bargain.

Proper Sizing

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

Bigger isn't better when it comes to HVAC. An oversized unit turns on and off too often, burns extra energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs constantly and still falls behind on the hottest afternoons. That's why we never just copy the tonnage of the old unit. Instead, we run a real load calculation that factors in your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

Choosing new equipment is about more than a SEER2 number. We'll compare heat pumps against a furnace-and-AC pair, and ducted against ductless, laying out operating costs, electrical and venting work, and the installation itself. Growing families frequently do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds more even temperatures across the whole house and uses less energy. The decision is yours, based on your home and budget.

Repairs We Handle

Heating and Cooling Problems We Solve in Wilson

Homes in Wilson run into a familiar set of heating and cooling failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Wilson HVAC handles them all: no cooling, air that blows but never gets cold, furnaces that won't ignite or short-cycle, frozen evaporator coils, refrigerant leaks, condensers that hum without starting, thermostats that lose their settings, clogged condensate drains, and mini-splits flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A weak capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a backed-up condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. We come prepared to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, repair leaky ducts that dump conditioned air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak is fixed.
